Separate the causes · historical diagnosis

Parser: the shared parser reads month first for day-first tenants. A date such as 7 April becomes 4 July. Keep the parser reports together across app, email and the booking-v2 form. Reports from booking-v3 tenants through app or email still belong here. The required fix was parsing according to the tenant’s date contract and verifying invite round trips. Track the five unusual bodies above as unresolved evidence within this ticket.

Form hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY while the form reads dates in the tenant’s day-first order. Users follow the displayed hint and submit a date the form interprets differently. This affects form reports for Alder Physio, Copper Studio and Pale Harbour. The proposed remediation was to align the hint and form validation with the tenant’s locale. A parser fix alone would not correct that instruction. Engineering instead retired booking-v3.

The exact report IDs and reproducible count output are in evidence/analysis.json. See the repository README for publication status and both GitHub issue links.
